What I actually do

Less "I leverage synergies," more: I build systems that move data from one place to another without losing or lying about it along the way.

I spent two years as an SAP Basis engineer at Accenture, on call for incidents that cost real money if they ran long. That job taught me to think about uptime, failure modes, and what happens when a pipeline silently breaks at 3am — nobody watching.

Now I build the thing I used to firefight: medallion-architecture pipelines (bronze → silver → gold), streaming ingestion with Apache NiFi, batch orchestration with Airflow and dbt, landing in ClickHouse and Postgres. Most of it containerized, most of it something I've broken and fixed more than once.

I'm currently deepening the statistics and ML side at TU Wien, while keeping one foot in production work.
